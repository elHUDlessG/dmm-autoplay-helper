# Changelog

## 1.6

Public source release of the helper previously labelled development build v16.

- Wait for a new mod enable before queuing mounting and launch.
- Do not launch merely because DMM opened with checked or mounted mods.
- Do not trigger launch from mod-folder file changes.
- Keep the four-second mod-enable delay and 35-second game-window-to-E timer.
- Keep the helper on top during startup, then minimize after the E tap.
- Include source, setup instructions, DMM attribution, and the MIT License.
